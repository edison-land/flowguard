# Evidence contract v1

review.json has version=1, target={commit,scope}, adapters (array), frontier (array), coverage (array), gaps (array), findings (array), decision={outcome,rationale,owner}. Every finding: id, claim, status (source-observed/hypothesis/reproduced/refuted), severity (critical/high/medium/low), confidence (high/medium/low), trigger, effect, evidence (nonempty array), counterevidence (array), boundary (source/component/integration/end-to-end/production), remedy. Evidence is {path,line_start,line_end,sha256}; hash the entire file at the reviewed working tree, not just HEAD. Paths are target-relative; line numbers are one-based, inclusive. Do not put source excerpts or secrets in JSON. `validate REVIEW TARGET` validates structure, paths, source hashes/ranges and minimum Go conditions; it does not verify the truth of a claim.

Gaps record scope, missing evidence, impact, owner (unassigned if unknown), due (unknown if unknown), next safe verification. Coverage declares inspected and not inspected surfaces. Frontier records trigger, question, action, observation, next/reason stopped; adapters declare actual mode and loaded resources. No universal feature taxonomy is mandated. Add fields freely for actual domain evidence. Deduplicate by mechanism and affected path, preserve conflicting evidence. Each finding's remedy is advice only.

Nested records are required: adapters={name,mode,reason,loaded_paths,input_evidence_ids,output_artifact,limitations}; frontier={trigger,question,action,observation,next}; coverage={inspected,not_inspected}; gaps={scope,missing_evidence,impact,owner,due,next_safe_verification}. Frontier and coverage must be nonempty.
